Plant Your Zanzibar Memory

Plant Your Zanzibar Memory lets you choose, buy and plant your own tree at Paje Farm Houses — a living connection to the island after your holiday. Care for it yourself on return visits, arrange paid care, and follow photo updates from home.

Questions about Plant Your Zanzibar Memory

Does the plant belong to me?

Yes. The programme is designed for you to buy and own a plant kept at Paje Farm Houses, with ownership and care terms confirmed before purchase.

What happens when I return home?

Arrange paid maintenance and follow progress through photos and care updates.
