Samaki wa Kupaka na Wali wa Nazi

Samaki wa Kupaka is the signature dish at Paje Farm Houses: grilled fish in a coconut-tamarind sauce, served with coconut rice, kachumbari and lime. It is a classic Swahili coastal plate — order it as part of a restaurant reservation in Paje, on the East Coast of Zanzibar.

What is samaki wa kupaka?

Samaki wa kupaka is grilled fish coated in a tamarind-coconut sauce. At Paje Farm Houses it comes with coconut rice, kachumbari and lime.
