Pilau ya Zanzibar na Kuku wa Kienyeji

Pilau ya Zanzibar with kuku wa kienyeji is fragrant island pilau rice served with slow-cooked local chicken, kachumbari and pilipili on the side. It is one of the most filling Swahili plates at Paje Farm Houses in Paje, Zanzibar.

Questions about Pilau ya Zanzibar na Kuku wa Kienyeji

What is Zanzibar pilau?

Zanzibar pilau is spiced rice cooked with island spices. At Paje Farm Houses it is served with slow-cooked local chicken (kuku wa kienyeji).

Is the chicken local?

Yes. The dish is prepared with kuku wa kienyeji — local chicken — and the team confirms availability when you enquire.
